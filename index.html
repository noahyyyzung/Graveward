HAYMAKER

VR (Quest 3): hold GRIP and point your fist to fly. Reach out farther to go faster, and speed builds up over time. Use both grips to go faster still, and press A to boost. Swing your fists to punch. TRIGGER grabs enemies by the neck, cars, or chunks ripped out of a wall; let go mid-swing to throw. Press X in the air to slam the ground. Left stick walks; right stick turns, or moves you up and down while hovering. Turn your left wrist up for the menu, then point at it and pull the right trigger.
Desktop test: click to look, WASD/Space/C to move, Shift = fast, click = punch, 1–6 spawn enemies, T = time of day, R = reset.
