HAYMAKER

VR (Quest 3): hold TRIGGER and point your fist to fly (reach farther = faster, both triggers = faster). A = sonic boost. X in the air = ground slam. Swing your fists to punch. GRIP grabs enemies by the neck, cars, or chunks torn out of walls; let go mid-swing to throw. Left stick walks, right stick snap-turns or moves you up and down while hovering. Turn your left wrist up for the menu, then point and pull the right trigger.
Desktop test: click to look, WASD/Space/C to move, Shift = fast, click = punch, 1–6 spawn enemies, T = time of day, R = reset.
